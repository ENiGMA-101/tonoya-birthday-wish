# শুভ জন্মদিন, তনয়া

A cinematic birthday gift from a brother to his younger sister — **তাবারাকা হাসান তনয়া**.

Not a party template. A small digital story: teasing, fighting, family, and a quiet *I love you* at the end.

## Flow

Cake with lit candles → blow them out → Happy Birthday, তনয়া → come inside → sibling story.

Default language: **বাংলা**. Toggle `বাংলা | ENG`.

All copy and photo paths: `src/config/birthdayConfig.ts`  
Photos: `public/images/birthday/` (profile, family, sibling, tour).
